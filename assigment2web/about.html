About Lana Del Rey

Biography

Elizabeth Woolridge Grant, known professionally as Lana Del Rey, is an American singer-songwriter. Her music is known for its cinematic quality and themes of tragic romance, glamour, and melancholia, with references to pop culture and 1950s–1960s Americana.

Quick Facts

  • Born: June 21, 1985 (New York City)
  • Genres: Sadcore, Indie Pop, Baroque Pop
  • Breakthrough Track: "Video Games" (2011)
  • Debut Major Album: Born to Die (2012)

Musical Style & Vintage Aesthetic

Lana’s distinct sound mixes orchestral arrangements, hip-hop-influenced beats, and slow retro vibes. Her visual aesthetic often incorporates vintage American flags, classic cars, Hollywood nostalgia, and retro film filters.

Iconic Quote

"Who are you? Are you in touch with all of your darkest fantasies? Have you created a life for yourself where you can experience them?"
